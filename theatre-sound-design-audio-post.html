SD.Live is an independent audio design and production studio led by sound designer Samuel David Llano. The work connects storytelling, playback, system design and final mixing for theatre, cruise entertainment, live performance and recorded content.

01 / THEATRE SOUND DESIGN

Theatre Sound Design

Creative and technical theater sound for live productions, built around the story, the venue and the way the show needs to run from rehearsal through performance.

  • Sound effects, atmospheres and transitions
  • Playback design and QLab programming
  • System and console programming
  • RF, FOH, monitors and cue workflow
  • Rehearsal support, documentation and technical handoff
  • Project scope shaped around the production, schedule and deliverables
02 / AUDIO POST

Audio Post

Audio post-production for recorded and branded work, shaping dialogue, effects and ambience into a clear, intentional final mix.

  • Dialogue editing and cleanup
  • Foley, effects and atmosphere editing
  • Transitions, deliverables and final mix
03 / INTERNATIONAL

International Collaboration

Theatre and cruise work can combine remote preparation with on-site rehearsals, programming and production support wherever the project is built.

  • Project-specific creative and technical scope
  • Remote preparation and on-site support
  • Travel, accommodation and per diem agreed with the production

Frequently asked questions

Planning an international production

How is international sound design quoted?

Every project is quoted around its creative and technical scope, production size, schedule and required deliverables.

Are travel costs included?

Flights, accommodation and per diem are agreed separately according to the production schedule and location.

Can preparation happen remotely?

Yes. Remote preparation can be combined with on-site rehearsals, programming and production support as the project requires.

Do you work with QLab, playback and show control?

Yes. Services include QLab programming, playback design, cue workflows, show control and technical handoff.
